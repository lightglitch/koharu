---
title: 'Export PNG, PSD, and CBZ'
description: 'Export every page as flattened images, layered documents, or a comic archive.'
icon: 'file-export'
---

Review the pages before choosing **File → Export Project**. Export always writes every page of the project, in project order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a format">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="PNG">
        One flattened image per page for delivery and review, drawn as the canvas draws it.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="PSD">
        One layered Photoshop document per page for further typography or artwork edits. Every layer stays editable: the scan is the bottom pixel layer, panels and balloons become folders, and each text block becomes a folder holding its clean-up as a pixel layer masked by the block's mask, its source as a hidden type layer, and its translation as a type layer. Visibility, opacity, blend modes, and locks are kept. Pages larger than 30,000 pixels on a side cannot be exported as PSD.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="CBZ">
        One comic archive of the flattened pages, for readers that open CBZ files. Before you pick where to save, choose how its pages are encoded: PNG (lossless, largest), JPEG (smallest, transparency flattened onto white), or WebP (smaller than PNG, keeps transparency). JPEG and WebP quality are set under **Settings → Export**, from 1 to 100 (90 and 85 by default).
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a destination">
    For PNG and PSD, select a folder in the native dialog. Files are numbered in page order with at least three digits:

    ```text
    001.png
    002.png
    ```

    For CBZ, choose where to save the archive; it is named after the project by default, and its pages are numbered the same way. Existing files with the same names are overwritten. The **Activity** panel shows how many pages are written; **Stop** ends the export before its next page, and deletes a CBZ it leaves unfinished, as it does one that fails.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  PSD is an interchange format. It does not keep model provenance, undo history, or Koharu's
  automatic fitting. Keep the `.khr` project file as your editable source and backup.
</Note>

## Check consistency

The canvas, PNG, CBZ, and PSD all draw through the same renderer. If they differ, report the page, format, and images through [troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting).

There is no separate “inpainted image only” export.
